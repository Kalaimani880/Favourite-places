Favourite Places

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in India. It is located in Agra and was built by Shah Jahan. It is known for its beautiful white marble architecture.

Golden Temple

The Golden Temple is located in Amritsar, Punjab. It is one of the most beautiful religious places in India. The temple is famous for its golden architecture and peaceful environment.

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is located in Mysore, Karnataka. It is famous for its beautiful architecture and historical importance. It is one of the most popular tourist places in India.
